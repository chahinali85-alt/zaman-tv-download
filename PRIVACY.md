# Zaman TV – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 24 November 2026_

Zaman TV is a TV player for phones, tablets, computers and TVs. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Zaman TV has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect, store or sell any personal data, and we have no servers that receive information from the app.

## What stays on your device

- Your favourite channels, recently watched channels, settings and parental-lock PIN.
- IPTV or M3U links you add yourself, including any username and password in them.

This information is saved only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

## What the app connects to

- **Public channel lists**, such as the free iptv-org lists on GitHub, to show the built-in channels.
- **The TV streams you choose to watch**, and any IPTV service whose link you add. Those services receive the normal information any video request includes, such as your IP address. Their own privacy policies apply.

Zaman TV does not host or provide any TV channels or content.

## Children

Zaman TV is not directed at children under 13. It includes an optional parental lock.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
